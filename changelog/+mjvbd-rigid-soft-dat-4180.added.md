Add experimental rigid-soft Divide-and-Truncate to the kinematic full MJVBDV2 backend, adapted from Newton PR #4180. The opt-in `vbd_options` setting `rigid_soft_enable_dat` constrains dynamic rigid and particle updates using detection-time contact planes. The PiPER bag demo enables it for evaluation and adds a sphere-surface penetration check. Contact-aware Chebyshev is supported with warmup, polishing, and contact-neighbor exclusion; DAT-clipped particles are excluded from subsequent extrapolation. The PiPER demo enables this combination at 15 iterations and 10 substeps to improve suspension. Multilevel acceleration remains incompatible. The demo also enables edge/face contacts against an SDF of the original ball mesh to cover coarse cloth triangle interiors.
