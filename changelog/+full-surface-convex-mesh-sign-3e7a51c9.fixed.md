Fix inside/outside misclassification of full-surface face-vertex and edge-edge contacts against convex-hull meshes, when the parity ray from the soft point passed through shared mesh edges or vertices (the wrong side, and so an inward contact normal, could differ between CPU and CUDA). Convex, consistently wound, single-shell hulls without sliver faces now classify points by the nearest feature's angle-weighted pseudonormal, which is exact and avoids the ray cast. Contact signs, normals and the resulting `SolverCoupledHighAccuracy` and `SolverVBD` trajectories can change where the old parity result was wrong; other meshes keep the parity query.
