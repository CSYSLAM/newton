Allow CUDA particle tile solves in MJVBDV2 when small color groups coexist with larger groups, and replay runtime IK through CUDA Graphs in the PiPER paper-bag example. Tile execution can change floating-point rounding and activate explicitly configured tile-only surface optimizations; set `particle_enable_tile_solve=False` to retain scalar execution. CPU execution and models whose groups are all smaller than a tile retain their existing path.
