Add `particle_enable_translation_correction` and `particle_bending_yield_angle` to `SolverVBD`. The translation correction follows every particle iteration with a coarse Newton step that moves each connected soft body, together with the free rigid bodies resting on it, as a whole, so thin stiff shells hold a grip or carry a load without crumpling. The yield angle makes bending hinges elastoplastic, so paper-like sheets keep creases and dents.
