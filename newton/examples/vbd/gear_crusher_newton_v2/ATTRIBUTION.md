# Asset attribution

## Stanford Armadillo

`assets/source/armadillo_stanford_original.obj` is the `armadillo.obj` model
published in the `libigl/libigl-tutorial-data` repository:

`https://github.com/libigl/libigl-tutorial-data/blob/master/armadillo.obj`

That repository
attributes the model to the **Stanford 3D Scanning Repository**:

`https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/`

The generated files `armadillo_stanford_surface.obj` and
`armadillo_stanford_tet.npz` are simplified, tetrahedralized, reoriented, and
scaled derivatives used for this research demo.

Consult the Stanford 3D Scanning Repository's model-use terms before
redistributing or using the model outside research/education contexts.

## Crusher drums

`crusher_drum_18t.obj` and `crusher_drum_18t.npz` are newly generated geometry
reconstructed from Figure 8 of the DAT paper. They are not extracted from the
paper and are not claimed to be the authors' original unpublished mesh.
